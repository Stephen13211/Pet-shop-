Returns & Refunds Policy

At Paws Incorporated, we want you to be happy with your purchase. This policy explains how we handle returns, defective products and refunds for orders placed through pawsinc.co.za.

Change-of-mind returns

We do not accept returns simply because a customer has changed their mind or no longer wants the product.

Defective or incorrect products

If you receive a product that is defective, damaged, incorrect, or otherwise does not meet the applicable requirements, please contact Paws Incorporated as soon as possible after receiving your order.

Please provide your order details and photographs or other information showing the issue where appropriate. We will assess the matter and provide the appropriate resolution in accordance with applicable South African consumer law.

Refunds

Where a refund is applicable, the refund will be processed using the appropriate payment method or another agreed method, subject to the circumstances of the order and applicable law.

Exchanges

We do not offer exchanges for change-of-mind purchases. Where a product is defective, damaged or incorrect, we will determine the appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable South African consumer law.

Contact

If you have an issue with an order, please contact Paws Incorporated with your order details so that we can assist you.
